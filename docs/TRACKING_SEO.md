## SEO

- The app page is indexable (`index,follow`) with title, meta description, canonical, `hreflang` for Greek and English, Open Graph with a preview image, and two JSON-LD blocks (`WebPage`, `FAQPage`). The English URL is served with English head tags and an English `FAQPage`.
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` are generated by the app.
- The Webflow page carries title, description and Open Graph in its page settings. The free plan does not allow custom head code or sitemap control, so the structured data lives on the app page.

**AI search.** The page answers a buyer's questions in plain, complete sentences and repeats them as `FAQPage` markup, so an assistant can quote an answer with its source. Product names are written exactly as on uphellas.gr, and every claim is limited to what the tool does, which keeps generated answers from inventing prices or tax treatment. The next step would be one short, citable paragraph per product on when it fits.
